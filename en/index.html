What to expect

How a session works

A session is structured individual work with the body, directed at chronic tension, restricted movement and accumulated load in the tissues.

We begin with an assessment of the main tension zones and how the body is currently building compensations. The work is carried out manually and continuously adapted according to tissue response and structural changes.

Where appropriate, gradient vacuum techniques and sensory attunement are integrated — for deeper release and stabilisation of state.

The process does not follow a fixed protocol. It follows the actual state of the body in the moment.

Before you book

Consider whether this
is right for you

If you experience sustained physical tension, pain patterns or movement restrictions that don't resolve fully with standard approaches — this is for you. But if you are looking for a quick massage, general relaxation or a spa treatment — this is not the right place. This is precise professional work with specific structural and functional disorders, and it requires time and the body's readiness for change.

First step

How it begins

After your request we will briefly review your situation — to understand whether this format of work fits your case. If it does, we confirm the session time and give basic recommendations on preparation and what to expect at the first visit.

The first session is devoted to assessment and initial structural work. Deeper changes develop gradually in subsequent sessions — depending on the body's response.
