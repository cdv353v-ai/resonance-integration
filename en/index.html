Each session is shaped around the specific person. We sense the state from the first minute — mood, tension, energy — and choose what is needed for today.

At the start of the session the person holds a tuning fork in their hand. A strike — and the instrument begins to sound. Sound and vibration at 432 Hz enter the body through the palm. The person hears this and feels it at the same time. 432 Hz is not an arbitrary frequency. It is the frequency on which medical tuning forks are based. The nervous system perceives a steady rhythmic signal and begins to anchor to it. The body shifts from "stress and defence" to "rest and recovery" — and this happens before any hands-on work has begun.
II
Music · Holding the frequency
The entire session takes place with music tuned to 432 Hz. It begins sounding together with the tuning fork and does not stop until the work is complete. This is not background noise or a relaxation playlist. It is a constant sound anchor. While the session continues, the music holds the state the body entered through the tuning fork. The nervous system does not have time to return to its habitual mode of tension. It stays open. This is precisely what allows the work to reach a deeper level. The body is not defending itself. It is listening.

Structural diagnosis and correction in a body that is already prepared. Correction in a state of readiness reaches deeper and produces more lasting results.

Questions about your visit

Frequently asked

Where to stay near you?+
The closest accommodation options are in Calheta and Jardim do Mar, 5–10 minutes by car. There are small hotels and apartments with ocean views. We can suggest specific options after booking.
Do I need a car?+
Yes. Prazeres is on the western coast of the island. Public transport here is infrequent. A car is the only convenient way to get here and move around the island freely.
How do I get to Madeira?+
Direct flights from most major European cities. The airport is in Funchal, from there it is about an hour by car to Prazeres along the expressway. For most guests, flying is the most convenient option.
When is the best time to visit?+
Madeira is beautiful year-round. Average temperature 18–24°C, with no season of extreme heat or cold. For a recovery programme, spring and autumn are the quietest times.
Do I need a visa?+
Portugal is part of the Schengen Area. Citizens of EU countries, the UK and most English-speaking countries enter without a visa for up to 90 days. Citizens of Russia and other CIS countries enter on a Schengen visa.
What languages do you work in?+
Russian and English. Over the last three years our practice has been almost exclusively with Russian- and English-speaking clients.
How far in advance should I book?+
For a programme of 4 sessions — at least two weeks in advance. Dates are reserved after advance payment. For an initial session — 3–5 days ahead. Urgent cases are considered individually.
