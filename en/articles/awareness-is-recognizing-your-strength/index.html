Awareness and Body

Awareness is recognizing
your own strength

May 5, 2026 · Olga Vdovskikh

Awareness is recognizing your own strength. About how the practice of awareness meets the body. Resonance Integration, Madeira.

This is exactly what people seek when they go to a Vipassana retreat — when they get up at five in the morning, sit on a mat, stay silent for ten days, breathe to a special count. All of this works because at some moment the mind tires of its own running and quiets down. And then, for the first time, what lies beneath becomes audible.

But there is another path. It does not cancel the first one — it complements it. The body.

The body is never in the past or future. It is always now. The breath you return to. The feet touching the floor. The touch that reminds you: you are here. The sound on which the body itself begins to align, without your understanding how or why.

When the body steps out of defense mode, when the nervous system is no longer on alert, when breathing becomes deep, the inner noise also stops. Not because you defeated it, but because it no longer has reason to spin.

This is the most direct path into the here and now that we know. And this is what we work with.

Who comes to us

Different people come to us. With different pain, from different countries, in different languages.

But over six years of joint practice, we have noticed one thing. Those with whom the work goes deep are united not by diagnosis or age, but by the fact that they already observe themselves. They have already asked themselves: what is happening to me. They have already tried different things — meditation, retreats, breathing, therapy, movement. They already know that body and state are connected, even if they cannot yet articulate how.

We do not teach awareness from scratch. We work with those who already have it, and we help the body catch up with consciousness. Because often consciousness is already where a person wants to be, but the body still holds the old: old contractions, old patterns, old ways of defending itself.

Our work is to remove this misalignment. So that what a person already knows about themselves on the inside becomes true on the outside as well. In posture. In breath. In how they stand on their feet.

Different worlds

We do not believe that everyone should arrive at the same place.

Each person has their own world, which they created themselves — through their attention, their choices, their history. And this is right. Therein lies the dignity of an aware person: they live not "like everyone else," but in accordance with their own tasks and their own inner structure.

Therefore, the most valuable thing that can happen between people is not merger, but meeting. When two different worlds find points of contact and from these points begin to create a shared space. A space in which each one remains themselves.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is awareness in simple terms?
It is a state of presence in the current moment, without the mind's automatic reactions. When you notice what is happening to you right now — in the body, in the breath, in feelings — and you do not drift into the past or future.
How are awareness and body work connected?
The body is always in the present moment — it physically cannot be in the past or future. Therefore, work with the body, breath, and nervous system is the most direct path into the here and now, especially when the mind is overwhelmed and will not let go.
Do I need to be advanced in practice to come to a Resonance Integration session?
No. But we have noticed that the work goes deepest with those who already observe themselves: who practice, read, have been on retreats, ask themselves questions. The experience of self-observation helps to notice more quickly the changes that happen after a session.
What happens to a person after a session?
The body continues to change for several days. Many notice that it becomes quieter inside, easier to breathe, simpler to make decisions that previously felt stuck. This is not our doing — it is the work of the person's own system, for which conditions have been created.
