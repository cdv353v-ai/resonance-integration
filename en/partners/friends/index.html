Referral Programme

We were very glad to help you, and we are delighted that you can now become our partner.

Our way of saying thank you for supporting us is returning that care. Your health and your recommendations are what truly matter to us. Introduce three people to our practice who come and complete a session, and your next visit is entirely on us.

How It Works

Three steps — completely transparent

01
You share the word
Tell a friend, colleague, or family member who values a mindful approach to health about us.
02
They attend their session
Your guests book, attend, and pay for their initial visit. They simply mention your name when scheduling.
03
We contact you
Once three people from your recommendation have completed and paid for their visits, we will reach out to schedule your complimentary session.
What is included in the session

A full session (45–60 minutes) that includes thorough posture and biomechanics diagnostics, manual correction, relief of acute or chronic pain, and restoration of natural movement patterns.

This session is technically and qualitatively identical to a regular paid visit.
