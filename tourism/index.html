The environment is the first step of the method

Why here

Madeira is not a backdrop for the work. It is part of it. Levadas, ocean, mountains, air in which you slow down and breathe out, without rush or pressure. Here the nervous system shifts into another mode before we have done anything. Simply because the person has arrived in a place where nothing needs to be controlled.

This is the first condition of the method. After that the hands work, the sound works, the vibration works. But the environment has already done half the work.

Practical details

What to know in advance

Where to stay
We are not a hotel. Guests stay in Calheta, Prazeres, Ponta do Sol, Funchal — anywhere within a 30–40 minute drive works. On request we send a list of vetted options: boutique hotels, villas, ocean view apartments.
Do you need a car
Recommended. The island is small but mountainous, public transport is limited. If you don't drive, Uber and Bolt are available.
How to reach us
Caminho Lombo dos Barros, 21, Prazeres. About 40 minutes by motorway from central Funchal.
When to come
Year-round. Madeira has no sharp seasonality. December–March is rainier; April–November is sunnier with ocean swimming.
Visa
Portugal is part of the Schengen area. Most European passports require no visa. Others — standard Schengen visa.
Languages
English, Russian, Portuguese. Sessions run in whichever language is comfortable for you.
